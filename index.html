About Vorton Advisory LLC

Executive HR Advisory

Vorton Advisory LLC is an executive HR consulting firm providing discreet, strategic guidance to family offices, private households, startups, and growing organizations across the USA and internationally.

Founded by Sonia Lebron, SPHR, with more than 20 years of HR leadership, Vorton Advisory brings Fortune 500 rigor and private household expertise to every engagement.

Whether you are building HR infrastructure for the first time, navigating a complex employee situation, or seeking retained advisory support — we deliver practical solutions with complete confidentiality.
